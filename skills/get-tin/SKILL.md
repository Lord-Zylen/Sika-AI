# SKILL: Get a TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number)

## Trigger
User asks how to get a TIN, or needs one for business registration,
opening certain accounts, or government transactions.

## Context to establish first
Ghana has integrated TIN issuance with the Ghana Card — most
individuals no longer need a separate TIN application if they have a
valid Ghana Card, as the Ghana Card number now functions as the TIN
for many purposes. Confirm this is still current before stating it
definitively; if a live/RAG source is available, check it.

## Steps
1. Ask if the user already has a Ghana Card.
   - If yes: explain that in most cases their Ghana Card number serves
     as their TIN for tax and business registration purposes, and
     confirm with GRA if a specific transaction requires separate
     verification.
   - If no: first step is Ghana Card registration through the National
     Identification Authority (NIA), which then enables TIN
     functionality.
2. For businesses needing a distinct business TIN (separate from the
   owner's personal TIN), explain this is handled through GRA alongside
   business registration.
3. Point to GRA's Taxpayer Portal or nearest GRA office for account
   activation/verification.

## Guardrails
- This area has changed in recent years (Ghana Card/TIN integration) —
  always flag that the user should verify current procedure with GRA,
  and use live retrieval if available rather than relying purely on
  memory.
