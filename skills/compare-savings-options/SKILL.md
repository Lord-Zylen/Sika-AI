# SKILL: Compare Savings Options

## Trigger
User is deciding where/how to save: susu, MoMo goal savings, bank
savings/fixed deposit, or cooperative/credit union.

## Required Inputs
1. Roughly how much they want to save and how often (daily/weekly/
   monthly)
2. What they're saving for and the rough timeframe (emergency fund,
   short-term goal, longer-term goal)
3. Whether they currently have a bank account, MoMo account, or neither
4. Priority: easy access vs earning interest vs safety vs discipline
   (not touching it) — ask if unclear

## Comparison Framework (present as a table)
| Factor | Susu | MoMo Goal Savings | Bank Savings/Fixed Deposit | Credit Union (CUA) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Interest earned | None (may charge a small fee) | Little to none typically | Low but present; fixed deposits higher | Often better rates than banks, dividend-based |
| Access speed | Fast, cash-based | Instant, phone-based | Slower, may have withdrawal terms | Moderate |
| Discipline/lock-in | High — collector visits build habit | Medium — can be withdrawn easily unless locked | High for fixed deposit | Medium-high |
| Documentation needed | Minimal | Phone/SIM registration | Ghana Card, proof of address | Membership + ID |
| Safety/protection | Depends on collector's reliability; susu companies are more regulated than individual collectors | Backed by telco + BoG e-money rules | Ghana Deposit Protection Corporation covers eligible deposits | Regulated under CUA framework |
| Best for | Daily/market-day savers, building trust-based discipline | Tech-comfortable users wanting quick, flexible saving | Larger sums, goals needing safety + growth | Members of a trade/community group wanting better returns |

## Steps
1. Ask clarifying questions above before recommending a comparison.
2. Present the table filtered to only the options relevant to the
   user's situation (don't overload with all 4 if 2 clearly fit).
3. Explicitly name trade-offs — e.g., "Susu is easy to start and builds
   discipline through the collector visiting you, but you generally
   won't earn interest and there's a small risk if the collector isn't
   trustworthy — this is why choosing a registered susu company or bank-
   linked susu scheme is safer than an informal individual collector."
4. Never declare a single "best" option — end by summarizing which
   option best fits which stated priority.

## Guardrails
- Do not recommend a specific susu collector, bank, or company by name
  unless the user names one and asks about it specifically.
- Do not guarantee interest rates or returns — direct to the specific
  institution for current rates.
