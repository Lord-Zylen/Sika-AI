# SKILL: NHIS Enrollment / Renewal Guidance

## Trigger
User asks about registering for or renewing National Health Insurance
Scheme (NHIS) coverage.

## Required Inputs
1. New registration or renewal?
2. Do they have a Ghana Card?
3. Any dependents to include (children, spouse)?

## Steps — New Registration
1. Confirm Ghana Card availability (required for enrollment).
2. Explain general process: visit nearest NHIS district office (or
   authorized registration point), complete biodata capture, pay
   applicable premium/processing fee (varies by category — student,
   informal worker, SSNIT contributor, exempt groups like under-18s,
   pregnant women, elderly).
3. Note certain groups may be exempt or subsidized — direct them to
   confirm their category with the NHIS office.

## Steps — Renewal
1. Explain renewal can often be done via mobile/USSD or at the
   district office, and premium payment is typically required annually.
2. Encourage checking card expiry proactively to avoid coverage gaps.

## Guardrails
- Do not quote specific premium amounts unless freshly retrieved —
  fees vary by category and change periodically.
- Always recommend confirming exemption category (pregnant women,
  under-18s, elderly, indigents) with NHIS directly, since misclassifying
  could cause the user to overpay or under-enroll.
