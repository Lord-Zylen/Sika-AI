GHANA VAT REFORM 2026 — VALUE ADDED TAX ACT, 2025 (ACT 1151)

The Value Added Tax Act, 2025 (Act 1151) took effect on 1 January 2026 and overhauled Ghana's VAT system. This is a major change from the pre-2026 rules and should be treated as the current position.

WHAT CHANGED:
1. VAT Flat Rate Scheme (VFRS) ABOLISHED. The former 3% flat rate on supplies of goods and the former 5% flat rate on certain supplies of immovable property were removed effective 1 January 2026. Businesses that used the flat rate (mainly wholesalers and retailers of goods) must now charge VAT at the standard rate.
2. Unified standard rate: VAT 15% + NHIL 2.5% + GETFund 2.5% = effective total rate of 20% on the same taxable value. (The pre-2026 effective rate was 21.9%.)
3. VAT registration threshold increased from GHS 200,000 to GHS 750,000 in annual taxable turnover.
4. Input tax credit: businesses on the standard scheme can claim input VAT, subject to the rules in Act 1151 and GRA VAT administrative guidelines.
5. Scope expanded to expressly include exploration of natural resources and export of non-traditional products (except cocoa, coffee, shea butter).
6. Zero-rating on locally manufactured textiles extended to December 2028.
7. Stricter penalties for failure to register for VAT.

PRACTICAL GUIDANCE:
- A retailer who previously paid 3% flat VAT must now register/remain registered, charge 15% VAT (plus levies), and can claim input tax credits.
- Flat-rate operators below the registration threshold can apply to the Commissioner-General to be deregistered.
- Existing flat-rate taxpayers who do not meet the threshold should remain registered until formally de-registered by the Commissioner-General after audit.
- Records must support input tax claims; deductions are not allowed twice or for undeclared purchases beyond six months in transition.

For Sika AI: when a user (trader, shop owner, contractor) asks "how much VAT do I pay?", the answer under current law is the standard 20% stack (15% VAT + 2.5% NHIL + 2.5% GETFund), NOT the old 3%/5% flat rates. Cite Act 1151 and note the effective date of 1 January 2026. Always direct users to gra.gov.gh to confirm their registration status and threshold.
